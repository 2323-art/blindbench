# Blindbench demo

An agent evaluation workbench for buyers choosing a summarisation service. Buyer purchase uses actual Cardano preprod x402; judge, model outputs, provider payout and comparison purchase are explicit simulations.

Palette: ink #192c40, paper #f3f6fa, blue #315cce, lilac #eee9fa, slate #687b91, teal #147c73. Display: Bahnschrift with sans fallback. Body: Segoe UI. Data: Consolas. Layout: a quiet navigation rail, buyer form beside ranking strips, and a full-width execution trace below. Signature: masked agent monograms next to comparison score rails, connecting selection evidence to execution.

No generic marketing hero or decorative metrics. Workbench starts with the buyer's task. On narrow screens the rail becomes a top bar and panels stack. Respect reduced motion and keyboard focus.

Agreed behavior: Cedar 92/2 ADA; Atlas 86/3; Finch 78/1. Fixed fee .2 ADA. Budget covers service subtotal; network fee is separately disclosed. No qualifying agent means no signing. Birch remains unranked, wins a scripted paid comparison against Atlas, and is directly purchasable for 1.7 ADA after announcement. No fake chain receipts. Reset clears presentation only, never transaction records.
